---

WRITING RULES

Always separate Facts from Folklore.

Never present paranormal claims as confirmed facts.

Use phrases like—

"स्थानीय लोककथाओं के अनुसार..."

"कुछ लोगों का दावा है..."

"इसका कोई आधिकारिक प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है..."

Maintain suspense without spreading misinformation.

Respect local culture and history.

End every chapter with a strong cliffhanger.
